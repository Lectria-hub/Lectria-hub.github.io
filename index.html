I’m an MRes student in Drug Design at UCL. I work on protein binders and small molecules, using structures to decide which designs to take forward.

In my current work on nanobodies, I compare how different models place the same candidate on a receptor. I’m particularly interested in designs where the models disagree.

Selected research

Protein design

Nanobodies for NK2R

I design single-domain antibodies for the neurokinin 2 receptor. After changing the antibody loops, I compare the predicted contacts across models and check whether the proposed binding site is still accessible when receptor glycans are included.

Crick Data Challenge · 2026

Metal sites in protein structures

For our team’s AtomFlow project, I curated seven metal-containing complexes. I checked ligand charges and recorded which atoms coordinate each metal, then repeated the preparation to verify that both runs produced identical structure files.

Virtual screening · Summer 2026

A shortlist for HSV-1 DNA polymerase

Starting with about nine million compound records, I built a screening workflow to select 100 candidates. I compared docking poses across six polymerase structures and kept track of chemical scaffolds so that close analogues wouldn’t dominate the final list.

UCL undergraduate project · 2025–26

Drug targets in rare kidney diseases

I studied ten proteins implicated in atypical haemolytic uraemic syndrome and C3 glomerulopathy. Comparing disease evidence with known compounds and available structures helped me narrow the list to five targets for further small-molecule research.

UCL Network Physiology Lab · Summer 2025

Patterns in blood oxygen recordings

I used MATLAB to analyse 20-minute oxygen saturation recordings from 200 patients with cirrhosis. I measured how irregular each signal was and tested whether those measures carried information about prognosis.
